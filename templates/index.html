What gets recorded

For every analyzed frame: who should be in view, who was recognized, the result, the similarity, how many faces were found, the face number, speed, processing time, your labels and the current settings.

The CSV only has numbers and labels, never pictures.

Results are counted per frame. Frames right after each other are nearly the same, so they aren't really separate tests.

Help

What every page, button and number means.

Camera

Start camera turns on your webcam and starts looking for faces. A green box means the face matched someone you saved. An amber box means it didn't match anyone closely enough.

Take 10 samples saves one picture of the face every half second. Turn your head slowly while it runs so the samples are all a bit different. Take 1 saves just one. Using a name that already exists adds more samples to that person.

Face number: a temporary number for each face while the camera is on. It can change when someone leaves the picture.

The % is how alike the face is to the saved samples. It is not the chance that the name is correct.

Recognition settings

SettingWhat changing it does
Match thresholdHigher: fewer wrong names, but real people show as unknown more often. Lower: more matches, including some wrong ones.
Runner-up marginHigher: the best match must beat the second best by more, so close calls are rejected. Lower: close calls are accepted.
Processing widthSmaller: faster, but small or far away faces are harder to find. Bigger: more detail, but slower.
Look for faces onEvery 2nd or 3rd frame: less work for your computer, but names and boxes update more slowly.

Settings are saved in settings.json. They don't change the models or your saved samples.

People

Samples shows someone's saved face pictures. Rename changes their name and folder. Delete removes them and all their samples.

Import from photos: upload some photos and FaceTracker finds the faces and groups the ones that look alike. Use the menu under each face to move it to another group or leave it out. Untick Save to skip a whole group.

Scanning new photos replaces groups you haven't saved yet, so save first.

Benchmark

Who is in view? The person really in front of the camera. For a proper "nobody" test, make sure no one is in view at all.

Lighting, movement, distance and notes are just labels saved with the results. The app doesn't measure them itself.

Download CSV saves every recorded frame as a spreadsheet you can open in Excel.

Privacy and limits

FaceTracker uses two OpenCV models: YuNet finds faces and SFace compares them. Face pictures and face vectors are saved per person in saved_samples/. Everything stays on your computer.

It can get things wrong with bad lighting, blur, side-on faces, people who look alike and small photos. Only save faces of people who said yes. This is a hobby project, not a security system.
